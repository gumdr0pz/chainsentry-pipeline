# Job Description — AML/CFT Specialist (Blockchain Forensics)

**Company:** Aras Integrasi Sdn Bhd — ChainSentry product line
**Title:** AML/CFT Specialist / Manager — Blockchain Financial Crime & Compliance
**Reports to:** Solutions Architect / Product Lead (ChainSentry), with direct line to COO for commercial matters
**Location:** Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (hybrid) — periodic travel to Ho Chi Minh City, Bangkok, Dubai; remote-friendly for the right candidate
**Type:** Full-time · Immediate
**Salary:** Competitive, commensurate with experience (Malaysian structure; regional allowance for travel markets)

---

## About us

Aras Integrasi delivers sovereign, on-prem blockchain tracing and E-KYC through **ChainSentry** — jurisdiction-first blockchain forensics for governments, financial-intelligence units, banks and licensed virtual-asset service providers that the US-cloud incumbents price out or structurally cannot serve. Our positioning is not "cheaper Chainalysis": it is *your cases never leave your jurisdiction*, plus MENA/SEA/BRICS **corridor coverage** the incumbents do not hold, plus deployments a sovereign customer can actually audit. We are currently assembling a ~120-entity design-partner roster across Malaysia, SEA, the GCC, Turkey, Egypt and Switzerland.

## Why this role exists

ChainSentry's commercial pipeline is deliberately compliance-led: regulators and supervised entities adopt us because our methodology is defensible in *their* courts and our data handling satisfies *their* laws. Today that judgment sits with engineering and the product lead. This hire makes it an owned discipline — the person who makes every pilot, corpus label, and regulator conversation compliant, credible and conversion-ready. The single largest program risk on our register is named: *"pipeline outruns product"*. This role is the primary mitigation on the compliance and credibility axis.

## What you will own

**1. Alpha design-partner program (first 90 days, heaviest lift)**
- Draft and negotiate design-partner / pilot terms: 90-day pilots, written-reference clauses, sanitized labelled-case contribution, **data-rights clauses** (our pilots buy their data, not just their logo).
- Ensure every pilot is lawful in its jurisdiction — data-transfer, secrecy and privacy handling (e.g. Swiss secrecy law for FIU/SAR-type data, UAE data rules, Malaysian PDPA + AMLA).
- Run pilot compliance reviews with engineering so labeled dispositions flow back into the corpus legally and cleanly.

**2. Regulatory & FIU engagement**
- Technical-compliance counterpart for central banks, securities regulators, FIUs and police financial-crime units (BNM, SC Malaysia, VARA/SCA, UAE FIU, CBB, SPK/MASAK, FRA Egypt, MROS-class bodies).
- Author supervisory-grade artefacts: policy whitepapers, consultation responses, "AML/CFT expectations for digital-asset service providers" guidance, methodology papers with **published precision/recall per heuristic, per chain, per corridor**.
- Support MENAFATF/FATF-adjacent credibility work (Travel Rule, Recommendation 15 positioning).

**3. Corpus & sanctions integrity**
- Own the sanctioned-address and watchlist labelling pipeline: OFAC/EU/UK/HMG lists, court-document addresses, ransomware trackers, takedown archives — deterministic seed labels, versioned and sourced.
- Run sanctions screening on corridor clusters and cross-border settlement rails; flag exposure before a customer finds it.
- Define and QA labelling taxonomy with the data team: every label must survive the question "where did this attribution come from?"

**4. Casework & court-grade output**
- Produce tracing reports at brief/court grade in MY, UAE and CH flavours: methodology section, source citations, limitations, reproducibility.
- Support live casework with law-enforcement design partners (mule chains, scam-officer networks, exchange off-ramps) during pilots.

**5. Commercial support**
- Answer RFPs/tenders and compliance due-diligence questionnaires as the named AML/CFT SME.
- Review customer AML policies/tools as the hook of our "day-one AML" bundles for newly licensed VASPs (Vietnam, Egypt, KSA-adjacent).
- Train pilots: run ChainSentry methodology workshops for compliance teams and investigators.

## What you bring (must-have)

- 4–8 years AML/CFT/financial-crime experience, of which ≥2 hands-on with **blockchain / digital-asset** crime: typologies, tracing, exchange risk monitoring, Travel Rule.
- Came from the buy side of this problem: VASP compliance, bank AML/CFT, FIU analyst, LE financial-crime analyst, or forensic/consultancy crypto investigations.
- Working fluency in at least one primary framework: FATF (R.15, Travel Rule), plus one of **Malaysian AMLA 2001 + BNM PDS/DPMS policy documents + SC digital-asset guidelines**, **VARA Rulebook**, **UAE AML/CFT Federal Decree-Law**, **Turkish Law 7518/MASAK**, or **Swiss GATA/AMLO**.
- **CAMS** (active or committed within 12 months); CFCS, ICA or equivalent a plus.
- Can write regulatory-grade English prose (you will submit public consultation responses); BM and/or Arabic a genuine advantage; Turkish/Vietnamese/Russian a differentiator.
- Comfortable opening a block explorer and following USDT-TRON through peel chains and mixers yourself — you don't have to code, but you must not need someone else to trace.
- Judgment under sovereignty optics: understands why a Swiss FIU, a Malaysian examiner and a Dubai regulator each say no, and what "proportionate supervision" actually means to each.

## Nice-to-have

- Prior design-partner / pilot / PoC program ownership in a RegTech or fintech.
- Published writing (methodology, policy commentary) or expert-witness / regulatory-consultation experience.
- Experience with incumbent tooling (Chainalysis/TrmLabs/Elliptic) — you will help us displace it honestly.
- Regional network: MY regulators, GCC consultancies/Big-4 forensic arms, SEA VASP compliance desks.
- E-KYC/fraud assurance awareness (deepfake document detection) — our G6 console.

## What we offer

- A seat at the table of a sovereign blockchain-forensics product while it is still being shaped — your methodology choices become the product's doctrine.
- Direct exposure to central banks, FIUs and national police units across three continents within year one.
- Real budget and authority over compliance posture (we will not hire you to write policies nobody implements).
- Travel, regional visibility, and a published-methodology reputation you can own publicly.

## How to assess (internal note — strip before posting)

Screen on: (1) one writing sample — a 1-page mock "AML expectations for a VN pilot-licensed exchange"; (2) a live tracing exercise on a public cluster; (3) negotiation instincts on a sample data-rights clause. Red flag: candidate who only knows seat-based incumbent workflows and insists corpus beats deployability — that is the exact objection our wedge exists to break.
