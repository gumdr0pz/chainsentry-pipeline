Closing the loop on the Dark Web Investigation course I attended at UPNM from 12 to 14 October, hosted at their Cyber, Electronics & Robotics Cluster (Pusat Kecemerlangan Sultan Ibrahim).

A consistent theme throughout was handling everything to an evidence standard — keeping things admissible, not just interesting.

Good group, engaged — the questions in the crypto-tracing session were practical, not casual. That tells me the military is building real capability in this area, and it's the same problem space our platform works in.

What the students learned. In plain terms: how criminals hide online, how to find and collect intelligence from those places, how to trace cryptocurrency from a crime to a person, and how to turn all of it into a case file that stands up legally. A complete cycle from discovery to courtroom-ready output.
