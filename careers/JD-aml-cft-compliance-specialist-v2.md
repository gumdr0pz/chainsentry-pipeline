## 1. About Us

Aras Integrasi Sdn Bhd is a Malaysian technology and security integrator with an established government and regulated-industry client base, including national security-agency deployments. **ChainSentry** is the company's sovereign blockchain-tracing and E-KYC product line: jurisdiction-first blockchain forensics for governments, financial-intelligence units (FIUs), banks and licensed virtual-asset service providers that US-based cloud vendors price out, cannot serve due to data-residency or sanctions optics, or leave blind on Gulf–Southeast Asia–BRICS correspondent corridors.

Three standards define the product and set the bar this role upholds: **sovereign deployment** (the customer's cases never leave the customer's jurisdiction, auditable on-prem or jurisdiction-locked hosting); **corridor depth** (measurable, sourced attribution on the USDT/TRON Gulf-to-SEA off-ramp graph the incumbents underlabel); and **auditability** (detection methodology, label provenance and per-corridor precision/recall published, not proprietary — every label defensible in the customer's court). ChainSentry is assembling a design-partner cohort of ~120 candidate institutions across Malaysia, Vietnam, Thailand, the Philippines, Indonesia, Singapore, the UAE, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Jordan, Turkey, Egypt, Lebanon, Morocco and Switzerland. **This role exists because a compliance-led go-to-market fails on compliance friction — and that friction is a discipline this company intends to carry in a named seat, not informally.**

## 2. About the Role

You will serve as Aras Integrasi's principal decision-maker on AML/CFT matters for the ChainSentry program — the technical-compliance counterpart to central banks, FIUs and police financial-crime units across three continents, and the internal authority that makes every pilot lawful, every corpus label defensible, and every court-grade report forwardable without editing.

The mandate is a named pair: **(1)** own the compliance posture of the alpha/design-partner program — pilot contracts and data-governance terms, jurisdictional lawfulness, sanctions/watchlist integrity of the attribution corpus; **(2)** own the company's written presence before regulators and supervisors — supervisory-grade papers, consultation responses, published methodology. A practitioner seat with program-ownership weight: hands-on casework and drafting in year one, first-line analyst leadership as the cohort scales. This role does not replace sales capacity; it makes sales compliant and pilots defensible, and the distinction is reflected in every objective below.

## 3. Key Responsibilities

### Design-partner program governance
- Draft, negotiate and administer the design-partner agreement suite: 90-day pilot term sheet, written-reference clauses, sanitised case-contribution terms, and the **data-rights clause** that treats a pilot's labelled dispositions as paid consideration.
- Maintain the pilot compliance matrix: legal basis, cross-border data categories, hosting location, retention schedule, destruction/return obligations — per active pilot.
- Pre-empt jurisdiction blockers: Swiss banking-secrecy and data-localisation handling; UAE federal + VARA/SCA data rules; Malaysian PDPA and AMLA 2001 handling for law-enforcement and reporting-institution data; Vietnamese and Egyptian pilot-licence documentation.
- Run structured compliance review before each pilot goes live; certify what corpus material lawfully returns to the shared corpus, sanitised to what standard, under which clause.

### Regulatory, FIU and law-enforcement engagement
- Act as technical-compliance counterpart to Bank Negara Malaysia (Financial Integrity/Payments), Securities Commission Malaysia, VARA (Dubai), SCA (UAE federal), UAE FIU, Bahrain CBB, Turkey's MASAK and SPK, Egypt's FRA and FIU, and Swiss FIU-class bodies.
- Author supervisory-grade artefacts: discussion papers, co-authored whitepapers, public-consultation responses arguing proportionate surveillance standards, baseline guidance such as "AML/CFT expectations for a newly licensed digital-asset exchange."
- Prepare and co-present capability briefings to investigator audiences — methodology and recorded public-data output as the standard; live demonstration only when the import pathway is demonstrable.
- Support MENAFATF/FATF-adjacent positioning: Recommendation 15, Travel Rule obligations and implementation practice in corridor states on the roster.

### Attribution-corpus and sanctions integrity
- Own the sanctioned-address and watchlist labelling pipeline — OFAC, EU, UK (HMG/OFSI), UN lists, court-document addresses, ransomware payment trackers, takedown archives — converted into deterministic seed labels, versioned, dated, source-cited.
- Define label acceptance criteria; run QA on machine-generated clusters: no label enters production without a provenance chain that survives "where did this attribution come from?"
- Perform sanctions screening on corridor clusters and settlement rails for design partners on request, with documented false-positive handling and escalation.
- Publish precision/recall methodology per heuristic, per chain, per corridor, computed on the design cohort's own labelled feedback — auditable-but-young is a differentiator only if its credibility holds, and this role owns that credibility.

### Casework and court-grade reporting
- Conduct blockchain tracing casework during pilots: mule-network mapping, scam-centre fund-flow reconstruction, off-ramp identification, mixer and peel-chain handling, cross-chain bridge hops.
- Produce tracing reports at brief and court grade — MY, UAE, CH flavours — with methodology statement, cited evidence, limitations, reproducibility appendix, and a plain-language executive summary an investigator can forward unedited.
- Support law-enforcement design partners through internal processes (for example Malaysian investigation-paper support on jointly agreed cases) within agreed data boundaries; build the report template library from the first three real cases.

### Commercial and bid support
- Serve as named AML/CFT subject-matter expert on RFP/tender responses and supplier due-diligence questionnaires; own the reusable compliance-answers library.
- Review prospective customers' AML policies, Travel Rule posture and tooling as the diagnostic hook of "day-one AML" bundles for newly licensed VASPs (Vietnam pilot cohort, Egypt first-approvals cohort).
- Support pricing conversations on compliance substance — what a proportionate licence covers, what an address-band includes, why seat-based incumbent pricing misaligns for supervised SMBs.
- Deliver methodology workshops and investigator training during pilots; cohort competence is a conversion driver.

### Product feedback
- Convert pilot friction into product backlog: data-handling gaps, report-format gaps, audit-log requirements, jurisdiction features enter the sprint plan through this seat.
- Sit in weekly corpus and pilot reviews with standing veto language on unlawful data movement — a recommendation this role makes, engineering is required to answer.

## 4. Authority and Decision Rights

- **Recommend-block authority** on any pilot whose data handling cannot be made lawful in the customer's jurisdiction; escalation to COO within 24 hours, recommendation recorded.
- **Acceptance authority** on corpus labels and sanitisation standards for returning pilot data.
- **Template authority** on the standard data-rights annex within COO-agreed parameters; material deviations route to legal review.
- **Named SME signature** on compliance questionnaires and methodology publications; product-lead countersignature on anything contractual.
