## 1. Why this role exists (one paragraph)
ChainSentry's engineering is real; its *trust* is not yet built. Buyers of forensics are MLROs, CCOs and
FIU officers who only trust vendors whose methodology survives a BNM examination, whose case files survive
a court challenge, and whose salesperson has personally filed the STR they're now being examined for.
This role is the bridge: one senior financial-crime practitioner who owns ChainSentry's compliance credibility
end-to-end — product requirements, evidentiary standard, regulator engagement, and design-partner trust.

## 2. Mission

to their board, to BNM/SC, and in court.
