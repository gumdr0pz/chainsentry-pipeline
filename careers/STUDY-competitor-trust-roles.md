# STUDY — How the Big-3 staff "trusted crypto-product" roles
Fetched 2026-10-15 via Ashby public posting APIs: **Elliptic** (25 live jobs), **TRM Labs** (111 live jobs). Chainalysis board not publicly API-served (Greenhouse/Ashby/Lever slugs 404; no Chrome on host for browser walk) — rated NOT FETCHED, excluded from claims.

## Why this study
Operator question: "Does the Jeff JD cover crypto? And ChainSentry is for the world, not only Malaysia." Method: read how the category leaders actually write their trust/advisory/expansion roles, steal the grammar that works.

## ELLIPTIC — Head of Enterprise Regulatory Advisory (New York)
The closest analogue to our role. Key language, verbatim patterns:
- Mandate: "senior voice on compliance and regulatory affairs," subject-matter expert on the jurisdiction's regulatory forces ("Clarity Act, stablecoin frameworks, FinCEN guidance, SEC and CFTC jurisdiction, state licensing")
- **Turns regulatory engagement into commercial impact**: "trusted advisor to existing and prospective clients… help them adopt [product] with confidence"
- Explicit 6-month outcomes, named artifacts: "supported early customer engagements cited as a key expert resource"; "built trusted relationships with key regulators (for example FinCEN and Treasury)"; "visible thought leadership (publication, panel, consultation, or testimony)"
- Requirement: **10+ years compliance/licensing/regulatory affairs, client-facing**, digital assets OR financial services
- Note the jurisdiction-portable template: NY version names US regimes; the SAME role exists per-office (offices: London, NY, DC, Miami, SF, **UAE, Singapore, HK, Tokyo**)

## ELLIPTIC — Head of Compliance Product (London)
Requirements that calibrate how much crypto depth they demand of trust-side leaders:
- "Depth in financial crime compliance **from the product side** (transaction monitoring, screening, KYC/EDD, investigations), built inside an exchange, bank, fintech or compliance-tech company"
- "**Working knowledge of crypto and blockchain as a baseline**; deep blockchain-analytics experience is a strong plus" ← the bar is working knowledge, not chain-native
- "AI-native… shipped AI or agentic capabilities to production" ← AI literacy is now a stated trust-role requirement
- Player-coach, zero-to-one track record demanded

## TRM LABS — geography/expansion pattern (111 jobs)
Geo expansion is staffed as **network-activation roles, not quota sellers**:
- "Business Development Executive, Financial Institutions (APAC)" (Singapore): "**You are not being hired to build a network from scratch. You are being hired to activate a network you have already earned.**" "not a traditional closing-quota sales role"; wants "deep credible network across APAC FI, ideally head-of-digital-assets-at-a-bank pedigree"; "already uses AI day-to-day"
- APAC spine = Singapore, covering EMEA-style region packs: "Customer Success Manager | EMEA", "Global Investigator (Triage) | APAC Singapore", "Senior All-Source Intelligence Analyst | APAC", "Deployment Strategist, APAC | Singapore" (+ Eastern Europe variant — same template per region)
- Dedicated crypto-native investigator track hired SEPARATELY from trust/advisory track: "Global Investigator — National Security", "Senior All-Source Investigator, Severe Harms", "GEOINT Analyst, Scams" ← chain depth lives in the intelligence team, NOT in the trust/advisory seat
- "Sales Enablement Manager, Onchain Compliance" ← even enablement is a named role

## SYNTHESIS — the category's actual formula for "trusted product"
1. **Two-track hiring.** Chain-depth (investigators, analysts) is a separate bench. The *trust* seat is bought from the buyer's world (FI compliance/regulatory), with crypto at "working knowledge baseline."
2. **Jurisdiction-portable template.** Same senior trust/advisory role cloned per region (Elliptic 9 offices; TRM per-region squads). The playbook travels, the person's earned network doesn't.
3. **Named-artifact KPIs.** 6-month outcomes are artifacts (engagements cited, regulator relationships, publications/testimony), not activity counts.
4. **Network-activation, never cold quota.** Both companies write the "not a closing-quota role" disclaimer explicitly.
5. **AI-native is now stated** in trust-role requirements.
