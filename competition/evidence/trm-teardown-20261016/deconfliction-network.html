FAQ

What is TRM Deconflict?

A free deconfliction product from TRM that helps verified law enforcement officers coordinate on crypto-related investigations by checking if others are working the same wallet addresses and sharing context.

How much does TRM Deconflict cost?

TRM Deconflict is free for verified law enforcement users.

Who can use TRM Deconflict?

Verified law enforcement personnel from local, state, federal, and international agencies can use TRM Deconflict.

Does TRM Deconflict include training?

Yes. Users get access to selected TRM Academy resources to learn crypto basics and investigative best practices.

How do I sign up from TRM Deconflict?

Complete the form below with an official agency email and to request access and complete verification. TRM will confirm eligibility and enable your account.

How does TRM protect my data?

TRM applies strict security, access controls, and auditing appropriate for a law enforcement environment. Only verified users can access TRM Deconflict.

How does the platform notify investigators when overlapping wallets or subjects are detected?

When a submitted wallet overlaps with another active investigation, TRM sends alerts to the relevant verified investigators and a secure channel to request coordination without exposing case files.

What specific data do agencies share, and what information is never exposed?

Agencies share only essential identifiers such as wallet addresses and limited metadata for deconfliction. Full case narratives, suspect/victim PII, investigative tactics, and operational plans never leave the originating agency.
