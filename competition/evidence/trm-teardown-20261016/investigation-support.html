SERVICES

Extend your investigative team

Get experienced crypto investigators without adding permanent headcount.  Choose the level of support your organization needs.

Embedded Investigator Support

Embed a dedicated TRM investigator with your team on a full-time basis, virtually, on-site, or hybrid. You get a subject-matter expert inside active investigations, and your team builds its own investigative capability alongside them. 

What they do: 

  • Develop leads and trace funds across blockchains
  • Build graphs, analyze behavior, and document attribution
  • Produce evidentiary and intelligence-ready case narratives
  • Support seizures, demixing, and identifying cross-chain swaps
  • Deconflict with other agencies working overlapping addresses
  • Train and upskill agency personnel 

Best for: sustained casework and capacity building

On-Demand Investigator Support

Access TRM's Global Investigations team on a flexible, hours-based basis without committing to a full-time embed. Apply surge capacity and specialized expertise to complex or time-sensitive cases. 

What they do: 

  • Conduct advanced tracing and demixing with written work-ups
  • Analyze and structure bulk datasets, including legal process returns
  • Participate as subject-matter experts in investigative and interagency calls
  • Review and validate in-house analysis and methodologies
  • Deconflict with other agencies working overlapping addresses
  • Provide attribution sourcing, including the underlying evidence behind an entity label

Best for: short-term specialist needs

Ask the Investigator Support

Engage senior TRM investigators through office hours sessions. Bring a case, a question, or a workflow you want a second opinion on, and get targeted expertise and coaching. Learn what techniques are being applied and why they are being applied.

What they do: 

  • Run case clinics and second-look analysis
  • Advise on investigative tradecraft and workflows
  • Share updates on emerging crypto typologies and threat activity
  • Advise on best practices authoring complaints, affidavits, and subpoenas 

Best for: targeted expertise and coaching
