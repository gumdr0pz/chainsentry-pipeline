FAQ

Which types of organizations benefit most from Chainalysis Data Solutions?

Chainalysis Data Solutions (DS) was built for agencies and institutions that need specific visibility into blockchain activity. Law enforcement and intelligence teams can search across multiple blockchains to detect illicit networks, uncover flows of funds, and target key threat actors. Regulators can use DS to monitor licensees, identify systemic risks, and streamline audit workflows. Financial institutions and service providers can enrich internal systems with blockchain intelligence, improving risk controls and operational awareness. Because DS is highly flexible, it adapts to each organization’s workflows, from compliance and supervision to intelligence gathering and enforcement action.

How does Chainalysis Data Solutions help me proactively detect and respond to threats?

Chainalysis Data Solutions provides direct access to all curated Chainalysis datasets. This enables customers to write bespoke SQL queries to identify patterns of illicit actors, search across blockchains for those patterns, and set customizable alerts to get notified on activity of interest, maximizing disruption and seizure opportunities. Ultimately, it provides proactive monitoring and alerting so you can stay ahead of illicit activity and take timely action.

Do I need to be technical or know SQL to use Chainalysis Data Solutions?

No. Chainalysis offers no-code query builders so you can drill down to the exact information you need! Additionally, we have a capability called Workflows, an interactive no-code experience that guides you through advanced blockchain analysis with a simple, fill-in-the-blanks style interface.

Can I automate workflows using Chainalysis Data Solutions?

Yes, building queries or using Jupyter notebooks you can automate routine, tedious workflows such as mutual counterparty analysis or demixing scripts, as a couple common examples.

What makes Chainalysis Data Solutions different and how do I know if I need it?

Chainalysis Data Solutions is our most flexible offering, giving customers direct access to the full suite of curated Chainalysis datasets. This allows teams to pair DS with products like Reactor or KYT to enrich investigations, power custom analytics, and address bespoke, non–workflow-specific needs that go beyond standard monitoring or alerting.
