# 01 — Activation Paths to Market

**Status:** Canonical v1 (2026-10-08) · Six paths, run as a portfolio: two primary, two supporting, two optionality. Each path carries its own kill criteria — paths are hypotheses.

Every path shares the same **non-negotiables**: (a) no live demo before G2 passes (Gate-Exception Protocol §7 for inbound); (b) every pilot contract carries the data-rights clause (ACT-20261008-002 hire owns drafting); (c) regulator seats get policy-grade handling, never tester terms; (d) we never pitch "cheaper Chainalysis."

## Path 1 — Home-Market Anchor (Malaysia) · PRIMARY

**Thesis.** Aras' existing government trust is the cheapest pipeline the company will ever have. Malaysia gives us: a live inbound demand event (PDRM JSJK briefing, Oct 2026), a supervisory cascade route (BNM/SC set expectations → banks and DAXs buy), and a domestic reference story that travels into every other market pitch. Malaysia is not the revenue market — it is the **proof market**.

**Mechanics.**
- **LE casework wedge (MY-01 PDRM MAPO/JSJK).** Convert the inbound briefing into a 3-case, 90-day casework pilot (mule chains, scam-officer networks, exchange off-ramps), methodology + recorded public-data output first, jointly published framing. Exit artifact: pilot offer with data-rights clause + direct-procurement justification path (security exemption) started early.
- **Supervisory cascade (MY-02 BNM, MY-03 SC).** Never sell to the regulator. Co-write the quiet supervisory artefact ("crypto-AML expectations for MY DAXs, with a sovereign tracing option"); submit a consultation response naming proportionate surveillance standards. The whole GTM is one examiner question in a bank audit: *"what tracing tool do you run?"*
- **Displacement cohort (MY-06 Luno, MY-07 MX Global, MY-08 Tokenize, MY-09 TnG, MY-11 Zetrix).** Door = the compliance officer carrying the SC deadline. First move = free flow-scanner audit on their existing Reactor/CSV exports, report back in a week. Pilot = 60-day inbound-flow monitoring priced under their renewal. Luno caveat: sign MY-branch scope only.
- **Distribution multiplier (MY-04 NACSA, MY-05 CyberSecurity Malaysia).** Existing MyFleet trust → national distribution and channel credibility, not direct revenue.

**ICP.** Malaysian supervised entities with SC/BNM compliance deadlines; national LE units with caseloads and no tracing stack.
**First accounts (roster IDs).** MY-01, MY-02, MY-03, MY-04, MY-06, MY-07, MY-08, MY-09, MY-11, MY-10 (existing Aras client).
**Economics [EST].** MY pilot ACV band RM 120k–400k/yr; government deployments carry mandatory local-support clause. Domestic anchor worth 1 reference + cascade, not 1 logo.
**Gates.** G1+G2 before any casework live-tooling; JSJK briefing proceeds under §7 protocol now.
**Kill/continue.** If 3 case-pilot offer + 1 DAX paid pilot not signed by wk12 → home market proves to be a services market; downgrade path to reference-farm only.

## Path 2 — Regulator-Listed Campaign (UAE/GCC first, then Turkey/Egypt) · PRIMARY

**Thesis.** Live licensing regimes create **cohort capex**: every new licensee must buy an AML stack in the same quarter. One regulator relationship therefore equals dozens of pre-qualified buyers, and "listed by the regulator" is a trust signal neither we nor the incumbents can fake. The regime is live now: SCA issued its first federal VA-platform licence (Bybit) in 2026; VARA's licensee base is established; Turkey's Law 7518 custodian wave and Egypt's FRA first-approvals cohort are standing up greenfield stacks with no incumbent entrenched.

**Mechanics.**
- **Approved-tooling listing.** Apply as registered technology vendor to VARA and SCA via the compliance-consultancy channel (ACT-20261006-005 partner doubles as route). Deliver a corridor credibility artefact first ("Gulf outflows traced" report) — incumbents are thin on MENA attribution; this is where a young corpus still wins.
- **Supervisory console.** Offer the regulator its own market-monitoring console (VARA/SCA/MASAK/FRA each get one framing) — the regulator supervises *with* us, licensees inherit us as "regulator-listed."
- **Day-one bundles for licensee cohorts.** VN pilot licensees, Egypt FRA first-approvals, Turkey custodian wave: stack + policy docs + filing support, timed to licence issuance and launch demos.
- **Displacement at branch level.** AE-08 Binance FZE, AE-10 OKX UAE, AE-09 Bybit UAE (new SCA licence ⇒ guaranteed tooling review): global HQ deals ≠ branch corpus needs; quote the branch.
- **One-door-many-markets plays.** TR-04 Paribu bought CoinMENA (up to $240M): one pilot = TR + GCC logos. AE-21 Emirates NBD majority-owns BitOasis: one door = bank AML desk + controlling shareholder of AE-11.

**ICP.** Regulators writing live VASP rulebooks; newly licensed or newly federalised VASPs; bank-owned exchanges; branches of global exchanges under local rulebooks.
**First accounts.** AE-01 VARA, AE-02 SCA, AE-03 UAE FIU, AE-11 BitOasis, AE-12 M2, AE-09 Bybit UAE, TR-01 SPK, TR-02 MASAK, TR-03 BtcTurk, TR-04 Paribu, EG-01 FRA, EG-02 first-cohort.
**Economics [EST].** GCC ACV band USD 60k–250k/yr mid-tier, address-band priced; regulator consoles are low-revenue, high-leverage (never priced as the deal).
**Gates.** No UAE campaign until G2 demo-able + local channel signed (ACT-005 deadline); VARA win ≠ SCA win — run as two relationships with different doors.
**Kill/continue.** If zero listing progress and zero licensee pilots by wk16 in a regime we named live → the regime's capex is going to incumbents; re-tier before more travel spend.

## Path 3 — Sovereignty Prestige Play (Switzerland) · SUPPORTING (2026 credibility → 2027 revenue)

**Thesis.** A Swiss institution using us neutralises product-origin objections everywhere else — including in the Gulf and in MY government procurement. The CH market itself is slow and conservative; we treat it as an **endorsement market**, played only through doors where our wedge is the product itself: data-residency ("cases never leave CH") and corridor coverage MROS/Fedpol can't buy elsewhere.

**Mechanics.**
- **Methodology-first, never a sales call.** Published detection methodology + open-sourced heuristics + Swiss-hosted instance quoted *as the product* (SAR-type data never leaves CH — hosting is the quote, not the software). Academic affiliation (ETH/Zug circle) recruited for court credibility (STRATEGY parity item 4).
- **FIU corridor pilot.** CH-03 MROS drowning in SARs → redacted-cluster corridor-enrichment pilot with SLA, routed through the Swiss AML-consultancy channel (X-01 VQF adjacency), not direct.
- **LE prestige anchor.** CH-01 Fedpol ICS via cooperation-channel adjacency [EST: weakest door in the family]; ACT-20261006-006 rule applies — no warm path by deadline → formally defer CH to 2027, no drift.
- **DLT-bank displacement.** CH-04 Sygnum, CH-05 SEBA–ABACUS, CH-06 Bitcoin Suisse: single-vendor Western exposure is a board-level risk they already price; CH-07 Incore cross-border settlement = sanctions screen as the opening wedge.

**ICP.** Secrecy-law-constrained institutions; DLT banks with board-level vendor-concentration anxiety; federal LE with cross-border corridor blind spots.
**Economics [EST].** CH hosting + licence premium tier USD 150k–500k/yr; 2026 revenue expectation ≈ zero — budget this path in credibility, not cash.
**Kill/continue.** ACT-006 rule stands: no warm Fedpol-adjacent path by its deadline → whole path defers to 2027, resources return to Paths 1–2.

## Path 4 — Channel-Led Expansion (Big-4 / SRO / consultancies) · SUPPORTING (starts wk1, pays wk12+)

**Thesis.** We will never hire our way into 17 markets. The firms that audit the regulators and write licensees' compliance manuals already own the trust and the delivery capacity; they need a product with a story their clients haven't seen yet. Channel revenue arrives late but carries no headcount.

**Mechanics.**
- **X-11 Big-4 forensic arms (MY + ME)** — they audit supervised entities; they resell tooling; sign a reference-delivery partner first in MY (Aras services arm is a credible peer-partner, not a sub-contractor).
- **X-01 VQF-class SRO schemes** — one Swiss SRO agreement = reach to hundreds of DLV members; bundle the day-one AML pack under their scheme branding.
- **X-02/X-12 SEA + MENA AML consultancies** — the VARA/SCA/MASAK door itself (Path 2 runs on this rail); pay listing/referral economics honestly.
- **X-14 freezone formation agents** — lead flow at licence-formation time, before compliance budgets exist.
- **Enablement kit (build wk1–4):** methodology paper, corridor report, white-label day-one AML template pack, address-band partner pricing sheet, one-page regulator-listing explainer.

**Economics [EST].** Channel margin 20–30% of ACV [ASSESSMENT]; first channel-sourced booking wk12–wk20 target.
**Kill/continue.** No channel-signed booking by wk20 → channel is a slow-revenue myth for us at this stage; keep listings for doors only, cut partner-program spend.

## Path 5 — Product-Led Evidence (corpus as marketing) · SUPPORTING from day one, cheap

**Thesis.** Our wedge is published methodology vs opaque incumbents. In markets where buyers must defend cases in their own courts, **evidence artefacts convert better than campaigns** — and every artefact doubles as corpus work, so marketing spend buys product moat at the same time.

**Mechanics.**
- **Quarterly corridor report** ("Gulf outflows traced", "SEA scam-officer off-ramps Q4-2026") produced from public data + our labelling engine; every named target on the roster receives it addressed to their desk.
- **Public-labelled case teardowns** of *public* incidents (exchange hacks, sanctioned-entity flows) — methodology on display, no customer data touched.
- **Reactor-compatible free flow-scanner audit** as the standing first-meeting gift (already strategy: value before trust).
- **Methodology paper + precision/recall dashboard** — once corpus QA is the AML/CFT hire's, this becomes a monthly rhythm; publish even when recall is humble (auditable-but-young, per Values §1).
- **Academic co-label** (Path 3 ETH/Zug adjacency) stamps independence on the numbers.

**Economics [EST].** ~0.2 FTE + travel; target: ≥15% of meetings-generated via artefact inbound by wk16 [ASSESSMENT].
**Kill/continue.** Artefacts producing zero inbound by wk16 → distribution problem, not content problem; fold into Path 4 enablement and stop standalone publishing.

## Path 6 — Adjacent-Wedge Bundle (E-KYC + RWA/tokenisation monitoring) · OPTIONALITY (evaluate at wk12 gate)

**Thesis.** Two adjacent wedges where the incumbents are absent or shallow, and where existing roster accounts would take the bundle in the same contract: (a) **G6 E-KYC/deepfake-document assurance** for VASP onboarding desks (Gulf mule-account crisis makes this live demand, and MY-01 casework keeps generating scam-onboarding evidence); (b) **RWA/tokenised-deposit monitoring** — UAE bank tokenisation cohort (AE-17 RAKBANK AED stablecoin approval, AE-27 Securitize ME) is a near-empty market.

**Mechanics.** Bundle into existing pilots as an add-on at design-partner pricing; do **not** cold-sell either wedge standalone before wk12. RWA monitoring requires product work beyond current gates — scope only if wk12 gate is green.

**Economics [EST].** Attach-rate target 20–30% of signed pilots by wk24; treat revenue as upside, plan without it.
**Kill/continue.** If pilot partners show no pull for either wedge by wk12 gate → park; revisit at 2027 planning. Bundling prematurely dilutes the core tracing story (RSK-20261006-001 variant).

## 7. Gate-Exception Protocol (inbound demand before G2)

1. Accept inbound briefings (e.g. PDRM JSJK) — awareness is allowed; live demo is not.
2. Brief on methodology + recorded public-data flow-scanner output only; state plainly that live demonstration follows product readiness (frames discipline as rigour, not weakness).
3. Exit artifact always = pilot offer with data-rights clause, not applause.
4. Log every exception against the ACT that owns it (ACT-20261008-001).
