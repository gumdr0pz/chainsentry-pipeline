## How to use this suite

1. Decisions in `03` §3 are the operator's to rule on — each has a recommended answer and a default-if-silent.
2. Weekly execution follows `02` §2 calendar. Every Friday, KPI tree in `03` §1 gets one line per metric; the wk4/wk8/wk12 circuit-breaker gates are read from STRATEGY.md §9 and are not negotiable by mood.
3. Path activation/deactivation is recorded in `01` §8 decision log — paths are hypotheses, and hypotheses get killed on evidence, not on attachment.
4. Outreach mass-sends stay gated behind G2 (Reactor/CSV import demo-able). Inbound demand (e.g. PDRM JSJK, CAAM) is handled under the Gate-Exception Protocol (`01` §7) — brief methodology and recorded output; no live demo until G2 passes.
