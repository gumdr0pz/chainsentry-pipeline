## 1. The strategic move

ChainSentry stops being positioned as a *blockchain platform that serves compliance teams* and becomes a **financial & blockchain platform**: a compliance spine for financial institutions with blockchain forensics as the investigative muscle underneath. Three forces converge to make this the right moment:

1. **The AML hire (Jeffri) is the living bridge.** JD v4 already makes him Fiato Malaysia's named compliance officer AND ChainSentry's trust authority. His methodology — regulate → requirement → artifact → proof — IS the financial-platform product surface.
2. **Fiato becomes the first live tenant.** Dogfooding: the compliance layer that takes Fiato exam-ready (JD Task 1, Day 30–90 measures) is simultaneously the financial platform's first deployment and its founding reference. No cold-start.
3. **The market is handing us the moment.** TNG Digital's Head-of-FCC seat (stack undecided, 6–12-month purchase window per PROSPECT-SIGNALS), Bybit KL's six AML postings naming incumbent stacks, and SEA banks' KL hubs staffing the investigations layer — the buying center for "financial compliance infrastructure" is hiring NOW.

The blockchain track is not demoted. Forensics/casework stays the defensible wedge (Paths 1–2). The financial track widens the ACV inside doors we are already knocking.
