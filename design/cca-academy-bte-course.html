How to get the most from it

Seven habits that separate readers from tracers.

1
Short, regular sessions

Twenty focused minutes a day beats a single three-hour binge. The material compounds.

2
Read the raw transaction

Every lesson pairs with a live tx hash. Open it in an explorer and find each field yourself.

3
Labels are opinions

Explorer tags are someone's claim, not proof. Learn when to trust them and when to re-derive.

4
Follow the hours, not the hype

Deposit timing patterns reveal more about an exchange than any marketing page.

5
Screenshot as you go

Evidence you did not capture at observation time is evidence you cannot cite later.

6
Respect the privacy line

On-chain visibility is not a licence for indiscriminate disclosure. Learn the boundary early.

7
Test on testnets

Break things on free networks before you interpret anything that matters.
